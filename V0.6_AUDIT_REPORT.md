## Remaining blockers / unresolved items

1. **464 F09 cells** (`V0.6_CHANGE_REGISTER.csv`, status `phase6.2_only -- UNRESOLVED`): Phase 6.2 reclassified these from `GENUINE`/`WORDING-ONLY` to `AMBIGUOUS`; the confirmed one-sided-missingness defect does not explain them (both sides' statuses are not one-sided-NA/NR/UNA-vs-real in these cases, by direct check). No alternative explanation has been verified. They are excluded from v0.6-prospective. If they are later found to reflect a real, separate defect, v0.6-prospective's Axis B Same/Mixed figures would shift slightly (all 464 are core-field F09, so they affect Axis A/B, not the strict-field F04/F16 statistics, which already match Phase 6.2 exactly).
2. **Decision not yet made, and not made by this package**, on whether one-sided NA and one-sided NR should ultimately be treated identically (as v0.6-prospective does, by extending the existing `UNA` precedent) or differently from each other (mirroring the codebook's existing symmetric NA-GENUINE/NR-GENUINE split). This is flagged in `V0.6_METHODOLOGICAL_CLARIFICATION.md` as a genuine open methodological question, not resolved by code inspection alone.
3. Per this task's explicit scope, the five-publication exclusion recomputation, manuscript edits, and the broader D12 (unreconciled field-level "evidence gap" figures) item from the Master Defect & Decision Register remain untouched and unblocked by this package — they are simply out of scope for this milestone.
